01. Introduction

Welcome to Ubankamer, an application developed and operated by Mon Reve Properties LLC ("Company," "we," "our," or "us"). We prioritize user privacy and are committed to safeguarding the confidentiality and security of any data handled by our application.

This Privacy Policy details how we handle user information when you access or use the Ubankamer application and related services (collectively, the "Services"). By using Ubankamer, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

02. Information We Collect

Ubankamer is designed to minimize data collection and prioritize local user control. We collect information in the following ways:

A. Local Data & User Inputs

Information entered into Ubankamer—such as financial records, category preferences, or application configurations—is stored locally on your device by default. We do not require account registration, and your entries are not transmitted to or stored on our servers.

B. Diagnostics & Usage Information

To ensure application stability, we may collect non-identifiable device metadata and technical crash diagnostics (such as OS version, device model, and error logs). This information cannot be used to identify you personally.

C. Direct Support Inquiries

If you contact customer support via email, we will receive your email address and any information provided in your communication to help resolve your inquiry.

03. How We Use Your Information

The information handled by Ubankamer is used solely to provide and improve the application's core functionality, including:

  • Enabling core financial tracking and local utility functions.
  • Maintaining app performance, diagnosing bugs, and fixing crashes.
  • Responding to user inquiries and technical support requests.
  • Ensuring compliance with applicable legal obligations.

04. Data Storage & Security

Local Device Storage: All user-generated records, calculations, and settings reside on your hardware. You maintain full ownership and control over your locally stored app data.

Security Safeguards: Mon Reve Properties LLC relies on system-level operating system sandboxing and standard mobile device encryption protocols to safeguard your stored information. We strongly recommend configuring passcode, fingerprint, or facial recognition protection on your physical device.

05. Third-Party Services

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.

Ubankamer may utilize third-party SDKs (such as Apple App Store Services or Google Play Services) strictly for app deployment, OS-level crash reporting, or standard analytics. These third-party services operate under their respective privacy policies, which we encourage you to review.

06. Your Rights & Choices

Depending on your jurisdiction, you have specific rights regarding your personal information:

  • Data Deletion: You can completely erase your data at any time by clearing Ubankamer's app storage in your device settings or uninstalling the application.
  • Access & Modification: Because data is stored locally, you can view, edit, or delete your entries directly inside the application interface.
  • Opt-Out of Analytics: You may disable anonymized crash diagnostics through your device operating system's privacy settings.

07. Children's Privacy

Ubankamer is not directed to children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under 13 in compliance with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). If we learn that personal data from a child under 13 has been collected, we will take immediate steps to remove such data.

08. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. Updated policies will be posted on this page with a revised "Effective Date". Your continued use of Ubankamer after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the modified Privacy Policy.
